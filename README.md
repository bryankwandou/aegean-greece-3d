# AEGEAN — A Little Corner of Greece

Interactive 3D Mediterranean coastal village built with Three.js, custom GLSL water caustics and Gerstner wave shaders, real-time cloth vertex physics, and Web Audio procedural soundscape.

## Interactive Chapters

1. **01 / Under the Lemon Tree (`Lemon courtyard`)**: Elevated rooftop and courtyard view between whitewashed Cycladic houses with terracotta pergola shadow stripes, climbing magenta bougainvillea, and swaying lemon branches overlooking the moored harbor boat.
2. **02 / The Little Harbor (`Little harbor`)**: Waterside rounded-cobblestone patio with weathered turquoise bistro tables, bowls of fresh lemons, folding cafe chairs, terracotta planters, and crystal-clear turquoise cove water.
3. **03 / Linen & Sea Air (`Linen terrace`)**: Sunlit western terrace with three pastel linen sheets billowing in real time on a wooden clothesline beside a gnarled Mediterranean olive tree, stone bench, woven wicker basket, and sea-glass demijohn bottle.
4. **04 / The Pastel Shutters (`Pastel shutters`)**: Weathered turquoise louvered window shutters framed under a shady lemon tree canopy overlooking the harbor bay.
5. **05 / The Bougainvillea Lane (`Bougainvillea lane`)**: Whitewashed masonry archway draped in magenta bougainvillea blossoms leading down stone-flagged steps toward the open Aegean horizon.
6. **Aerial Village Overview**: Full free-orbit camera across the interconnected Cycladic coastal village and limestone cliffs.
